# KINGAI OPS · Public Roadmap

## English

### Now
- improve server and infrastructure visibility;
- simplify safe installation, updates and recovery;
- strengthen security posture, inventory and operational clarity;
- keep local-first control and public documentation understandable.

### Next
- richer fleet coordination and multi-node views;
- stronger policy, approvals and team workflows;
- easier MSP and small-business operations;
- broader edge/IoT compatibility and device awareness.

### Longer term
- governed autonomous operations;
- enterprise/private-control options;
- deeper security and infrastructure intelligence;
- hosting, hardware, MSP and OEM ecosystem partnerships.

Roadmap items are directional until explicitly released and verified.

# 中文

### 当前重点
- 提升服务器和基础设施可视化；
- 简化安全安装、升级和恢复；
- 强化安全状态、资产发现和运维信息清晰度；
- 保持本地优先控制，并让公开文档更容易理解。

### 下一阶段
- 更丰富的 Fleet 协同和多节点视图；
- 更强的策略、审批和团队工作流；
- 更简单的 MSP 与小企业运维体验；
- 更广泛的 Edge/IoT 兼容和设备识别能力。

### 长期方向
- 受治理的自主运维；
- 企业/私有控制方案；
- 更深入的安全与基础设施智慧；
- 与主机、硬件、MSP 和 OEM 建立更广泛的生态合作。

路线图内容在正式发布和验证前均属于方向，不代表已经全面可用。
